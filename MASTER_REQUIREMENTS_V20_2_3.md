# MASTER REQUIREMENTS — V20.2.3 Multi-Business Enterprise Operations

## 1. Preservation
- Preserve all V8–V20.2.2 files/routes/exports unless explicitly listed as integration points.
- Preserve existing farms/flocks, veterinary, marketplace, marketing, booking, sales, managed operations and legacy modules.
- Provide upgrade and rollback packages.

## 2. Multi-business operating model
- Support platform HQ, office, managed company, feed factory, warehouse unit, medicine/feed/equipment/chick offices, marketing unit and managed farm business.
- Each business unit has code, name, type, hierarchy, address/logo/contact, manager/responsible user, contract, currency preferences, warehouse links and cost center.
- Staff assignments are scoped to business unit, role, farms and warehouses.

## 3. Farms and veterinary integration
- Link existing farms to business units without rebuilding the farm model.
- Support manager/supervisor/primary vet/backup vet references at the business-farm relationship.
- Office dashboards must surface veterinary open/critical cases for their linked farms.
- Existing V20.2.2 daily clinical loop remains preserved.

## 4. Customer 360 / Supplier 360
- Customer: identity, office, farms, assigned user, currency, credit limit/days, sales invoices, receipts, debit/credit ledger and statement.
- Supplier: identity, office, supplied categories, currency, payment terms, purchase invoices, payments, debit/credit ledger and statement.
- Credit-limit breaches create an auditable exception rather than disappearing silently.

## 5. Product and inventory master
- Shared product catalog for medicines, vaccines, feed, chicks, equipment, farm supplies, raw materials, finished feed, services and other items.
- Support kg/bag/ton/unit/box/liter/bottle/dose with bag-weight conversion where applicable.
- Every physical stock change is recorded as a stock movement.
- Support receiving, issue, transfer-out/in, adjustments, returns, reservation/release semantics.
- Prevent negative stock when configured.

## 6. Procurement and receiving foundation
- Purchase Order → receiving → goods receipt → purchase invoice → supplier payable → journal.
- Track ordered, received, accepted, damaged, rejected and shortage quantities.
- Receiving variances create exceptions and preserve evidence references.

## 7. Sales and customer settlement
- Sales Invoice → stock issue → customer ledger → cash/receivable → balanced revenue journal → COGS/inventory journal.
- Support cash and credit sales.
- Customer receipts post to treasury and AR with vouchers and journal links.

## 8. Supplier payments
- Supplier payments reduce AP, create treasury outflow, supplier ledger entry, payment voucher and balanced journal.

## 9. Treasury and currencies
- Support cashbox, bank and transfer accounts per business unit.
- Support SAR, USD, YER_OLD and YER_NEW with original currency, original amount and historical exchange rate in source transactions.

## 10. Financial core integration
- Reuse V20.2 double-entry ledger; do not create a second accounting engine.
- Add inventory/raw material/finished goods/WIP/in-transit/COGS/feed sales accounts when missing.
- Every business unit receives a financial cost center.
- Journals generated by V20.2.3 must remain balanced.
- Provide business-unit financial summary and link source documents to journal IDs.

## 11. Stock transfers
- Transfer Order → Dispatch → In Transit → Receiving Confirmation.
- Origin stock decreases only on dispatch; destination stock increases only on confirmed receipt.

## 12. Feed factory foundation
- Feed factory exists as a business unit with factory profile.
- Raw-material and finished-goods warehouses can be attached.
- Built-in starting raw-material master: corn, soy, premix, mycotoxin binder, salt, calcium, alfalfa, bran and oil.
- Allow adding new raw materials.
- Raw materials support nutrient profiles: protein, metabolizable energy, calcium, phosphorus, fiber and moisture.
- Define Starter/بادي, Grower/نامي and Finisher/ناهي feed product types.
- Full formulation/manufacturing/yield/loss is V20.2.4.

## 13. Dashboards and exceptions
- Platform dashboard aggregates business units.
- Business-unit dashboard includes farms, customers, suppliers, warehouses, sales, receivables, purchases, payables, inventory value, low stock, treasury, exceptions and veterinary case counts.
- Exception center includes credit-limit and receiving variance events at minimum.

## 14. Audit, scope and API
- V20.2.3 routes use V20.2.1 authentication foundation.
- Business access is restricted by global roles or active business-unit staff assignment.
- New domain writes create audit events.
- Production state remains environment-separated and bootstraps V20.2.3 cleanly.

## 15. UI
- Add a V20.2.3 multi-business workspace to Windows/Web.
- User can choose active business unit.
- Provide screens for units/contracts/farm links/staff assignments, customers/suppliers/sales, warehouses/products/purchases/transfers, finance/treasury, and factory foundation/raw-material nutrients.

## 16. Validation
- End-to-end semantic test must cover office + contract + farm + staff + customer + supplier + warehouse + purchase/receiving variance + sale/credit exception + receipt + supplier payment + transfer + factory foundation + balanced journals + validation.
- Previous V20.2.1 and V20.2.2 semantic tests must continue to pass.
- Preservation audit against V20.2.2 must pass.
